Code has a half-life, but systems often do not.

When teams optimize only for immediate delivery, maintenance cost is deferred to people who had no voice in the original decision. That is not just a technical concern. It is an ethical one.

Long-lived systems deserve durable contracts, clear ownership boundaries, and humane operational playbooks. If a system is likely to survive beyond the current team, we should design for future maintainers as first-class users.
