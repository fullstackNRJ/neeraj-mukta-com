Generative models are most useful when treated as lenses, not oracles.

A strong workflow starts with constraints: audience, format, desired tone, and acceptable error envelope. With those constraints, latent-space exploration can quickly produce divergent directions that a human can evaluate and synthesize.

The win is not automatic originality. The win is compressed iteration time.

## Practical framing

- Use models for breadth in early ideation.
- Use human editorial judgment for convergence.
- Build traceable decision logs for production work.

Creativity does not disappear in this process. It becomes more architectural.
